 AgentEdu — Proposed Unified Adaptive 
 Education Architecture 
 Full title 
 AgentEdu: A Unified Cognitive-Agent Architecture for Explainable, Teacher-Governed, 
 Closed-Loop Personalized Education 
 One-line concept 
 AgentEdu continuously constructs a learner's evolving cognitive state, 
 identifies the root causes of learning failures, simulates alternative 
 interventions, obtains appropriate teacher/safety approval, measures the 
 intervention's actual effect, and uses that evidence to select the learner's 
 next intervention. 
 The important addition is that we don't make the system merely: 
 AI detects weakness → AI recommends lesson 
 Instead, we make it: 
 Observe → Diagnose → Model → Simulate → Govern → Intervene → 
 Measure → Learn → Re-plan

 The  Digital Twin  changes after every meaningful learning  event. 
 It contains: 
 ●  topic mastery 
 ●  prerequisite relationships

 3. B. Misconception Root-Cause Graph 
 This is more useful than simply storing weak topics. 
 Example: 
--- PAGE 5 ---

 ↓

 ↓

 ↓

 ↓

 So the system doesn't simply conclude: 
 “Student is bad at recursion.” 
 Instead: 
 “The observed recursion errors are associated with a likely prerequisite 
 weakness in stack-frame execution.” 
 This lets your intervention target the  cause  , not  merely the symptom.

 6. Explainability engine 
 Every important AI decision gets an explanation object. 
 For example: 
 { 
 "decision": "Prerequisite lesson", 
 "target": "Stack frames", 
 "evidence": [ 
 "4 incorrect answers", 
 "2 repeated misconception patterns" 
 ], 
 "confidence": 0.87, 
 "reason": "Observed errors indicate a prerequisite conceptual gap", 
 "expected_goal": "Improve recursive execution understanding" 
 } 
 Your UI can turn that into: 
 Why was this recommended? 
 Because:  the learner repeatedly made errors associated with stack-frame execution. 
 Confidence:  87% 
 Expected goal:  improve recursive execution understanding.

 13. What makes this a strong project 
 You aren't building just: 
 Chatbot + quiz 
 You're building: 
 A stateful adaptive educational software system. 
 The key components are: 
 1. Persistent learner state

 15. Copyright positioning 
 For your copyright application, describe the work as: 
 An original computer-program implementation of a unified agentic 
 educational platform for adaptive and personalized learning. 
 Not: 
 “A newly invented method of education.” 
 The Indian Copyright Office states that copyright protects the  expression of an idea rather 
 than the idea, procedure, process, or method itself  ,  while computer programs are eligible 
 for copyright protection.  Indian Copyright Office  FAQ

 AgentEdu is a unified agentic educational software platform that 
 continuously models learner knowledge and misconceptions from 
 learning interactions, generates and evaluates personalized intervention 
 strategies, incorporates teacher governance and explainable decision
